OUR JOURNEY

About Vinayaka Jewellers

Founded in 1992 in the heart of Chikkamagaluru by Mr. Mukesh Kumar, Vinayaka Jewellers began as a small family showroom with one promise — honest gold, fair prices, and jewellery made to be passed down. Three generations later, that promise still shapes every piece we craft.

From temple and antique Lakshmi haarams to bridal Kanjeevaram sets and certified diamonds, our artisans blend age-old South Indian craftsmanship with BIS-hallmarked purity — served, always, like family.
